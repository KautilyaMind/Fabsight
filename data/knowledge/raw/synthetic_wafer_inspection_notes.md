# Synthetic educational wafer-inspection notes

> This is a synthetic educational document created for the FabSight simulation. It
> is not a real production SOP.

A wafer map is a spatial grid of die-level inspection or test states, not an ordinary
photograph. Groups of abnormal dies can form patterns such as center, donut,
edge-local, edge-ring, local, random, scratch, near-full, or none. Pattern names
describe geometry and do not reveal a physical root cause.

An edge-ring pattern concentrates abnormal die states around much of the wafer edge.
An edge-local pattern is more confined to part of the perimeter. A scratch pattern is
often visually linear, while a random pattern is more dispersed. Classification
confidence describes model output, not certainty that a production mechanism is
known.

Inspection review can compare the predicted class, alternative class probabilities,
map quality, repeated patterns, and relevant process-step context. When process risk
and visual evidence disagree, the conflict should be preserved for investigation
rather than automatically resolved. Retrieved references may suggest useful topics,
but they do not confirm a case-specific explanation.
