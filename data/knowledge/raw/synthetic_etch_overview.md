# Synthetic educational etch overview

> This is a synthetic educational document created for the FabSight simulation. It
> is not a real production SOP.

Etching is a broad semiconductor fabrication stage that selectively removes material
from exposed regions of a wafer. Earlier patterning steps define which regions should
be affected. Educational process-control discussions often consider uniformity,
selectivity, pattern transfer, repeatability, and inspection feedback. These concepts
describe general engineering goals rather than a detailed recipe.

An investigation of unusual etch results may compare current observations with
historical baselines, review equipment alarms and maintenance context, check whether
the observation is isolated or recurring, and examine inspection patterns. A wafer
edge abnormality may motivate review of edge handling, uniformity, masking, and
inspection behavior as possible areas to investigate. The pattern alone does not
identify or confirm a physical mechanism.

Cleaning, deposition, lithography, implantation, chemical mechanical planarization,
and inspection interact as conceptual stages in a manufacturing route. Evidence from
one stage should be interpreted with timestamps, measurement quality, and provenance.
